Resort Guidelines

Good Neighbor Etiquette
local_fire_department Campfire Safety & Firewood expand_more

Fires are restricted to designated campsite steel rings. Due to invasive pine beetle protection, outside firewood is prohibited. Please use kiln-dried bundles from the camp office ($7/bundle). Never leave flames unattended and always extinguish with water before retiring.

cruelty_free Pet Policy & Leash Etiquette expand_more

Pets must remain on a standard 6-foot leash at all times outside fenced zones. Waste bags and disposal bins are at every loop intersection. Never tie pets to trees or leave them barking unattended.

recycling Curbside Trash & Eco Recycling expand_more

Curbside pickup is daily at 9:30 AM. Place tied trash bags at the end of your driveway. Sort aluminum, glass, and clean cardboard at the green recycling pavilion near loop junction B.
