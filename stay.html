Resort Guidelines

Good Neighbor Etiquette
cruelty_free Pets & Dog Park expand_more

Magnolia is pet friendly. Keep dogs leashed outside the dog park, pick up after them, and keep barking to a minimum. Some breed restrictions apply, so check with the office before you book.

phishing Fishing Ponds expand_more

The ponds are stocked, with Adirondack chairs and porch swings along the banks. Ask the office about fishing guidelines when you check in.

group Adults-Only Section expand_more

Part of the park is reserved for guests 21 and over. Ask the office if you would like a site there.
